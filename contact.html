Frequently Asked Questions

What is your MOQ?

Our standard MOQ is 500 units for rigid boxes and 1,000 units for folding cartons to ensure competitive pricing.

Do you provide 2D dielines?

Yes. Once structural dimensions are locked in, our engineering team provides a 100% accurate 2D dieline for your designers.

How long is mass production?

Structural prototyping takes 7 days. Once approved, mass production takes 15-20 days depending on finish complexity.

Can you ship globally?

Yes. We offer split shipping to multiple global fulfillment centers direct from our factories.
